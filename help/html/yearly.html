Yearly Breakdown

The Yearly Breakdown checkbox shows one row per year — the average high, average low, and total precipitation for each occurrence of the window — followed by a trend estimate per decade for each series.

Year-over-Year
------------------------------------------------------------
Year   | Avg High     | Avg Low      | Precip Total
2021   | 25.5°C       | 17.9°C       | 288.0 mm
2022   | 25.8°C       | 18.1°C       | 402.0 mm
2023   | 25.6°C       | 18.0°C       | 356.0 mm
2024   | 26.1°C       | 18.4°C       | 380.0 mm
2025   | 26.3°C       | 18.5°C       | 371.0 mm
------------------------------------------------------------
  Avg high trend: +0.32 per decade (up)
  Avg low trend: +0.21 per decade (up)
  Precip trend: +18.5 per decade (up)

How the trend is computed

A least-squares straight line through the per-year values, expressed per decade. For example, "+0.32 per decade" means the average high rose about 0.32° every ten years across the analyzed window. Only complete occurrences are counted, and cross-year windows (Dec–Feb) are labelled by the year they start.

Read trends with care. Weather is noisy: a decade-scale trend from 5 years of data is barely a hint, while the same slope across 20 years is much more meaningful. Use Recent vs Baseline to test whether a shift is concentrated in recent years, and prefer deeper depths before drawing conclusions.

Command line equivalent

python weather_juice.py --city Durban --period Summer --depth 20 --yearly
