Recent vs Baseline

The sharpest question you can ask of historical weather: are recent years actually different from the decades before them? The comparison answers it directly by splitting the analyzed history into two windows and printing the difference for each metric.

Recent vs Baseline
------------------------------------------------------------
  Metric           Recent             Baseline           Change
  Avg high         26.1°C             25.6°C             +0.5°C
  Avg low          18.4°C             18.0°C             +0.4°C
  Precip total     384.0 mm           341.0 mm           +43.0 mm
------------------------------------------------------------
  Recent window: 2021-2025 | Baseline: 2011-2020

Where to find it

This view is a command-line feature:

python weather_juice.py --city Durban --period Summer --depth 20 --compare-recent 5
  • --compare-recent 5 — the most recent 5 complete years.
  • --compare-baseline 10 (optional) — years immediately before them (default 10).

The depth must cover both windows: --depth must be at least recent + baseline (5 + 10 = 15 in the example above; depth 20 covers it comfortably).

How to read the Change column

  • Avg high / Avg low — mean daily temperature difference between the two windows, in your chosen units.
  • Precip total — difference in the mean per-occurrence rainfall total.
  • A small change over a deep baseline is a stronger signal than the same change over a shallow one.
GUI users: the same insight is available by ticking Yearly Breakdown and comparing the early rows with the recent ones (see Yearly Breakdown).
