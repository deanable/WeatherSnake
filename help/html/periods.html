Choosing a Period

The Period selector picks which part of each year is analyzed:

PeriodWindow analyzed (southern-hemisphere seasons)X axis
SummerDecember – February (crosses the year boundary)months or day-of-year
AutumnMarch – Maymonths or day-of-year
WinterJune – Augustmonths or day-of-year
SpringSeptember – Novembermonths or day-of-year
Full YearJanuary – Decembermonths or day-of-year
Montha single calendar month (a month picker appears)months
Custom Rangeany day/month span you choose (From/To pickers appear)auto-chosen

Seasons cross year boundaries

Summer runs December through February. WeatherSnake groups each December–February occurrence together and labels it with the year it starts, so "2023" means December 2023 plus January and February 2024. The analysis always uses complete occurrences only — a December with no following January/February yet is excluded rather than skewing the averages.

Custom ranges

Choosing Custom Range reveals From and To day/month pickers. Examples:

  • 15 Nov – 28 Feb — the build-up to and heart of summer.
  • 1 Apr – 30 Sep — a southern-hemisphere wet/dry split.
  • 24 Dec – 2 Jan — the festive fortnight, year after year.

Ranges may cross the year boundary. If the span covers more than 31 days the chart switches to monthly averaging automatically (as does the single-month mode); otherwise day-of-year averaging is used.

Command line equivalent

python weather_juice.py --city Durban --period Summer --depth 10
python weather_juice.py --city Durban --start-day 15 --start-month 11 --end-day 28 --end-month 2 --depth 10
