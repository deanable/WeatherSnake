Monthly Average & Shared Y-Axis

Monthly Average

When ticked, the chart shows one averaged point per month of the window (for example: December, January, February). When unticked, it shows one averaged point per day-of-year (for example: 01-12, 01-13, …) — a much finer curve that makes short-lived spikes and shoulder-season shifts visible at the cost of a busier axis.

  • Tick it for season overviews and clean multi-month comparisons.
  • Untick it to hunt for specific dates — "how hot is 24 December, typically?"
For Month and Custom Range periods the mode is chosen automatically: windows longer than 31 days use monthly averaging, shorter ones use day-of-year values, and the toggle is disabled.

Shared Y-Axis

By default the temperature lines and the precipitation bars each get their own Y scale. When Shared Y-Axis is ticked, both sides use the same range, which makes the two series visually comparable — handy when rainfall and temperature clearly move together (or in opposition) across the window.

Command line equivalent

python weather_juice.py --city Durban --period Summer --depth 10 --monthly --unify-scales
