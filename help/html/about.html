About WeatherSnake

WeatherSnake — a historical weather analyzer.
Version: see Help → About (the window title also shows it).

WeatherSnake turns decades of daily weather into practical insight: multi-year averages, most-common conditions, typical ranges and extremes, year-over-year trends, and recent-vs-baseline comparisons — so you can reason about future weather by reflecting on the patterns of the past.

Credits

  • Weather data: Open-Meteo Archive API (ERA5/ERA5-Land reanalysis, Copernicus Climate Change Service / ECMWF), CC BY 4.0.
  • Built with Python, Tkinter, pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Requests.
