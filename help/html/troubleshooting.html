Troubleshooting

"Could not find coordinates for …"

  • Check the spelling, or try a nearby larger city — the geocoder needs a recognized name.
  • Add a region or country for ambiguous names (Richmond UK vs Richmond, Virginia).
  • Check your internet connection; the geocoding request is online.

"No data available for the given timeframe"

  • Confirm the depth and period combination makes sense — a depth of 20 needs 20 complete occurrences of the window.
  • Very new settlements or remote islands may have limited archive coverage; try a nearby larger location.
  • Try a different period to confirm the location itself returns data.

Requests are slow or fail part-way

  • Depth-20 fetches download ~7,300 daily records — allow a few seconds.
  • Corporate proxies or firewalls can block the API; the archive lives at archive-api.open-meteo.com and the geocoder at geocoding-api.open-meteo.com.
  • Open-Meteo is a free service with fair-use limits; wait a moment and retry.

Online chart export fails

  • Datawrapper needs a free API token from app.datawrapper.de (Settings & Account → API tokens). Click Datawrapper Token to enter it, or set the DATAWRAPPER_ACCESS_TOKEN environment variable (see Exporting Data).
  • The button verifies the token against the API when you save it — if Datawrapper rejects it you'll see the server's own explanation in the dialog.
  • "HTTP 401/403" almost always means the token is wrong, expired, or was deleted — create a fresh one and save it again.
  • "HTTP 429" / a mention of quota means the free tier's monthly limit was reached (500 chart creations and 1,000 publishes per month) — wait for the next month or upgrade the Datawrapper plan.
  • "HTTP 5xx" means the service is having problems — wait a moment and retry.
  • "Could not reach Datawrapper" / "Could not reach QuickChart" means the network request itself failed; check your connection and any proxy/firewall for api.datawrapper.de or quickchart.io.
  • QuickChart PNG needs no account; the only common failure is its per-minute rate limit (60 charts/minute on the free tier) — wait a minute and retry.

The window looks wrong or blank

  • Reopen the application — a failed fetch can leave the chart area empty; the status bar (above the buttons) shows what's happening.
  • Settings persist in ui_settings.json; if a bad setting breaks startup, close the app and delete that file — it will be recreated with defaults.
